## Start Here

Verified classroom bundle.

1. Open the [student guide](student-guide.md). Follow the steps in order.
2. Upload the six [student notebooks](../student), then create your own lakehouse.
3. Attach your default lakehouse and the published `env_forestops` Environment in every notebook.
4. Follow [Planetary Computer downloads in Fabric](imagery-download.md) for Lab 01. STAC is the default; manual download/upload is the fallback.
5. Complete each TODO, select **Run cell**, and inspect its validation. Stop at any exception or `[FAIL]`. Use the [solutions](../solutions) as answer keys.
6. Verify Gold SQL, save and reopen your native Map, then test and publish your Data Agent.
7. Follow the [workshop deck](../deck/woodlands-manual-workshop.pptx) alongside the guide.

The facilitator completes [Environment setup](environment-setup.md) once.
The bundle contains no deployment scripts, credentials or pre-attached notebook bindings.
